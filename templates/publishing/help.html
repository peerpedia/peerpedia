Find and read a paper

On Papers, search by title, author, or keyword. Select a title to see the abstract and whole-paper reviews. Choose Read paper for the accepted text. Reading is public.

Open Filters to choose an arXiv subject or sort by rating. Choose Search to apply your choices.

Read chapters on a phone

The contents start closed. In the top bar, tap Contents to see the chapters and the headings in the current chapter. Scroll inside the list to reach later entries. Tap Close contents at the top to return to the same spot, or tap an entry to jump there and close the list.

Choose Abstract in the reading toolbar to return to the paper’s overview. Choose Peerpedia Home to return directly to the list of papers.

Use Previous chapter and Next chapter to move through a paper. Peerpedia remembers your reading position in your account when signed in, or in this browser when signed out.

Upload a paper

  1. Prepare a Markdown file. See the writing guide for equations, diagrams, and references.
  2. Sign in with a verified account. Choose Upload paper, select the file, and fill in the publication details.
  3. Choose Preview paper to check the text and authors. Use Edit upload to change the details or replace the file. Your current file and details are kept when you go back this way.
  4. Choose a license, confirm your submission rights, and select Publish paper.

Upload a work with chapters

Keep the chapter files and shared bibliography together. Follow the CLI chapter guide to publish or revise them together.

For drawings in Markdown, see the diagram examples.

Suggest an improvement

Download the accepted Markdown from Publication details and edit it locally. Choose Propose a change, upload the revised file, and describe what you changed. Choose Preview proposal to inspect the changes, then Submit proposal. The accepted text changes only when the corresponding author accepts the proposal.

For a multi-chapter paper, clone the paper with the CLI. If the paper changes before submission, apply your changes to the latest accepted version. To add another snapshot to your pending proposal, follow Update a PR.

Review a paper

Under the abstract, choose Write a review or Sign in to write a review. Select one to five stars, write your assessment, and choose Publish review. Use Edit your review to update it later.

The overall rating is the average of visible reviews. A review applies to the paper as a whole, even after it is revised.

Read earlier versions

On the paper’s overview, expand Publication details below Read paper and choose History. Select Read this version beside an entry. Once there are two accepted versions, Compare versions shows their differences.

For pending, rejected, or withdrawn proposals, open Proposed changes under Publication details.

In the reader, choose Cite for a link to the exact version or a paper link that follows the latest accepted text. To cite a specific chapter or heading, choose the link icon beside it. See the writing guide for Markdown references.

Administrators can hide inappropriate material. For a copyright, privacy, or withdrawal concern, use the private rights request form.

Follow the conversation

Open your inbox from Account. It collects reviews, proposals, decisions, and comments on proposals you are involved in.

Open an update to visit the discussion or paper and mark it read. Choose Unread to catch up, or Mark all read to clear the list. These notifications stay on the website. They are not sent by email.

Your account

Create an account with a username and password.{% if google_login_enabled %} You can also choose Continue with Google.{% endif %} Your username is public and your email address is private. Verify your email and accept the submission and discussion agreement before contributing.

To submit local files with an AI assistant, open Account → Access tokens and follow Connect your account.

If a verification email does not arrive, check your spam folder and request another. Verification links expire after 24 hours. Use Forgot password? to recover access.

When something goes wrong

Recover a draft from the old editor

If you have saved drafts, open Account → Export old drafts to download your files.

A formula, reference, or diagram looks wrong

Compare your source with the writing examples, correct the file locally, and upload it again for preview.

I need to report a problem

Report a bug on GitHub with the page address, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Leave out passwords, access tokens, and private information. Contact details are on the About page.

Prefer to work in a local editor or with an AI assistant? The separate CLI guide covers installation, reading feedback, and submitting local files.
